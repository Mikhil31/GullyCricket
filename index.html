Why this exists

Kids, put down the phone. Come out and play.

A generation is growing up indoors. Hours that once went to cricket in the lane, kabaddi after school and gilli danda at dusk now go to a screen. Gully Cricket Trust was set up to reverse that, one taluk at a time.

We organise proper, refereed competitions on the grounds children already play on. Every match is a chance to be seen, and every taluk winner moves up. The talent is already in our villages; what has been missing is a path. We are building it.

Rules & eligibility at a glance

Play fair, play your age.

The short version of the full rules on the Championship page.

Read the full rules

  • Open to Indian residents, in their own category: boys, girls, men or women.

  • Under-18: every player below 18 on [cut-off date]. Up-to-25: every player 25 or younger on [cut-off date].

  • One team per player per category; teams play for the taluk where they register.

  • Bring age proof (Aadhaar, school ID or birth certificate) to every match.

  • No alcohol, tobacco or drugs. Over-age players disqualify the whole team.

Our inspiration

What we've set ourselves to be judged by.

Sir M. Visvesvaraya grew up in a village in Karnataka and went on to build dams, schools and colleges that changed lives far from any city. He believed that discipline and hard work, applied patiently, lift whole communities. That is the standard we've set ourselves: a tournament is only worth running if a child from a small village gets a real chance to be seen. We keep his portrait on our wall to remember that the measure is how many players we lifted, not how big the event looked.
