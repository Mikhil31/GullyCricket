Desi Games

National Desi Village Games Movement

Reviving India's traditional sports heritage. A proposed all-India championship of village games at taluk headquarters, for boys and girls teams up to 25 years, with Taluk, District, State and National levels.

  • Gully cricket

    Street cricket with local rules and a tennis or tape ball.

  • Kabaddi

    A raider holds breath, chants and tags opponents in their half.

  • Kho-kho

    A chasing team tags runners, taking turns from a crouched line.

  • Lagori (Pithu)

    Knock down a stack of seven stones, then rebuild it before being hit.

  • Gilli danda

    Strike a small gilli into the air with a longer danda (chinni dandu in Kannada).

  • Tug of war

    Two teams pull a rope to drag the other over the line.

  • Marbles (Kancha)

    Flick glass marbles to hit targets.

  • Chain game

    Tagged players join hands to form a chasing chain.

  • Bagh chal

    Board game of tigers against goats.

  • Chaupar

    Ancient cross-shaped board game with cowries or dice.

  • Matka phod

    A blindfolded player breaks a hanging pot with a stick.
