**Colours:** Navy #14224A · Deep Navy #0A1433 · Gold #F2C14E · Bronze #B8780F · Saffron #FF9933 · India Green #138808 · Ball Red #C8161D

**Fonts:** Oswald (Bold 700, SemiBold 600) for headings and logo text, Inter for body copy. Both are free on Google Fonts. The SVGs load Oswald from Google Fonts; install it locally before editing them in Illustrator, Figma or Inkscape.

**Clear space:** keep a quarter of the logo width empty on every side. **Minimum size:** 48 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print.
